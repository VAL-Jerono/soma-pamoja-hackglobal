# Literature review and data basis

This document records the basis for the data model. It distinguishes evidence from project proposals.

## Evidence used

The UNESCO Global Proficiency Framework for Reading supplies descriptor-level constructs for decoding, fluency, spoken-language comprehension, and reading comprehension across Grades 1 to 9. Because it is not a Kiswahili edition, Soma Pamoja records alignment as a written rationale rather than claiming language-specific validation.

The Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development publishes lower-primary curriculum designs for Grades 1 to 3, including Kiswahili. The exact curriculum edition and any grapheme sequence used for decodability must be pinned to the downloaded official document before being treated as a source-backed rule.

The EGRA Toolkit supports language adaptation and a component view of early reading that includes letter-sound knowledge, familiar-word reading, nonword decoding, oral reading fluency with comprehension, vocabulary or oral language, and listening comprehension. This supports keeping micro-items, connected passages, and facilitator-read comprehension as distinct content types.

A longitudinal Kiswahili reading study reports that nonword decoding and listening comprehension predict reading comprehension, with decoding a strong predictor. This supports collecting decoding evidence in addition to oral reading. It does not establish a universal Kiswahili words-per-minute benchmark.

## Project decisions supported by the evidence

The project will not claim a numeric fluency benchmark without a pinned source, date, language, population, and measure definition. It will treat the initial passage word limits as engineering constraints only. It will keep speech evaluation data separate from learner learning data. It will use adult proxy readers first and avoid describing that data as child speech.

## Source caveats

Secondary summaries were used for some proposed curriculum and benchmark observations. Those observations remain unresolved until the official curriculum and primary benchmark documents are available. No secondary WER or fluency figure is used as a project result.
